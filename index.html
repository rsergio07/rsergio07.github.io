About Me

I’m a Senior Site Reliability Engineer and Cloud Solutions Architect with over 20 years of experience building secure, resilient, and automated infrastructure at scale. My work sits at the intersection of reliability, automation, and technical mentorship.

I specialize in Kubernetes, Terraform, Ansible, Docker, and CI/CD pipelines — tools I use daily to create repeatable, high-performing environments across hybrid and cloud-native platforms. I also lead onboarding programs, mentor aspiring SREs, and design internal training paths to grow strong, self-sufficient engineering teams.

I believe great systems are not just reliable — they’re teachable. I enjoy sharing what I learn through workshops, blog writing, and public talks. Outside of tech, you’ll find me enjoying time with my family, following football, hiking, or catching a good film.
